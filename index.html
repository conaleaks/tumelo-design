My Process

How I Get & Serve Clients

Transparent, collaborative, and straightforward — here's how we go from first contact to final delivery.

1

Discovery & Referrals

New clients come through direct referrals from satisfied past clients, social media presence on Instagram (@wise_academy_sa), online freelancing platforms, and word-of-mouth within professional networks.

2

Initial Consultation

We discuss your project goals, requirements, timeline, and budget via WhatsApp, email, or video call. This ensures complete clarity before any commitment is made.

3

Proposal & Agreement

I provide a detailed written proposal outlining the scope of work, deliverables, pricing, and timeline. A deposit is required to begin work.

4

Design & Delivery

I deliver drafts for review, incorporate feedback through revision rounds, and finalize all files. Payments are processed securely and efficiently.

5

After-Care Support

I remain available after project completion for minor adjustments and ongoing consulting, building lasting professional relationships with every client.

Policies

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, fair terms that protect both you and me throughout our collaboration.

💰 Refund Policy

  • A non-refundable deposit of 50% is required to begin any project.
  • Cancellation before work begins: full refund of the balance (deposit is retained).
  • Cancellation after work begins: partial refund based on work completed.
  • No refunds once final files are delivered and approved by the client.
  • Refund requests must be submitted in writing within 7 days of delivery.

🚫 Cancellation Policy

  • Clients may cancel at any point by providing written notice via email or WhatsApp.
  • Cancellations before project start: full balance refund (deposit retained).
  • Cancellations mid-project: invoiced for work completed to date.
  • TUMELO DESIGN reserves the right to cancel if communication ceases for more than 14 days.
  • All design assets remain property of TUMELO DESIGN until full payment is received.

🔄 Revision Policy

  • Each project includes up to 3 rounds of revisions at no extra cost.
  • Additional revisions beyond included rounds are billed at an hourly rate.
  • Major scope changes after approval may require a revised quote.
  • Revision requests must be submitted clearly and in a single round where possible.

📦 Delivery Policy

  • Estimated timelines are provided at the start of each project.
  • Delays caused by late client feedback do not extend the delivery guarantee.
  • Final files are delivered digitally via email or cloud storage link.
  • Source files may be provided upon request for an additional fee.

For policy enquiries, contact tumelodesignsa@gmail.com before placing an order.
