Refund and Cancellation Policy

Trading Name: TUMELO DESIGN • Principal: Tumelo J Leriba • Gateway: Paystack • Updated: October 2026

At TUMELO DESIGN, led by Tumelo J Leriba, we are committed to delivering high-caliber digital solutions, visual graphic design, and strategic consulting for clients in South Africa and international markets. All client transactions and invoice settlements are securely processed via Paystack.

Client Satisfaction Commitment:
We aim for 100% satisfaction on every creative project. This policy clearly outlines your consumer rights regarding cancellations, revision rounds, and refund procedures for our custom digital deliverables.

1. Scope of Digital Services

Our services encompass custom digital solutions and creative consulting, including but not limited to:

  • Brand visual identity design, corporate logos, and vector asset suites.
  • Social media promotional kits, advertising creative, and campaign collateral.
  • Digital positioning audits, consulting reports, and presentation pitch decks.

2. Project Cancellation Policy

2.1 Cancellation Prior to Project Commencement

If a client requests a project cancellation within 24 hours of payment and prior to the commencement of any design drafting or consulting preparation, the client is entitled to a 100% full refund of the amount paid, minus standard third-party payment gateway transaction fees where applicable.

2.2 Cancellation During Active Milestone Work

If a cancellation request is filed after initial creative concepts or project roadmaps have been prepared and delivered:

  • Initial Concept Phase: If the client elects not to proceed after reviewing preliminary design drafts and does not wish to utilize revision rounds, a 50% partial refund will be issued to compensate for the creative labor and hours expended.
  • Final Approval Phase: Once final revisions are completed and approved by the client, fees are non-refundable as the project has reached completion.

2.3 Completed Projects and Downloaded Assets

Once final high-resolution vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF) or finalized strategy reports have been delivered and downloaded by the client, no refunds can be issued due to the digital and irrevocable nature of downloadable intellectual property.

3. Complimentary Revision Policy

To guarantee creative alignment without needing a cancellation:

  • Every commissioned project package includes 2 to 3 rounds of free revisions to adjust colors, typography, layout balance, and proportions.
  • Additional scope additions beyond the initial agreement are quoted transparently upfront before proceeding.

4. Refund Processing Timelines via Paystack

💳 Paystack Gateway Processing:
All approved refunds are initiated directly through Paystack back to the client's original funding instrument (Credit Card, Debit Card, or Bank Account via Instant EFT).
  • Review & Authorization: Refund requests are evaluated and processed by TUMELO DESIGN within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Settlement to Account: Once authorized via Paystack, funds typically reflect in the client's account within 5 to 10 business days, governed by standard inter-bank clearing cycles.
